Privacy Policy

Push-Up App ("Crush & Protect") · Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short
  • Your camera image is analysed on your iPhone only. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple and our payment partner RevenueCat.

Camera

The app uses your front camera to detect your push-ups. Each camera frame is processed in real time on your device with Apple's on-device body pose detection (Apple Vision). Frames and body landmarks are never saved, uploaded or shared, and leave your device at no point.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, the name you give your buddy, your push-up counts, streaks and game progress are stored locally on your iPhone. We have no access to them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules a daily reminder as a local notification on your device. No data is sent to us for this.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. To manage your subscription status we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and basic technical device data (such as app version, iOS version, country and language). This is necessary to provide the subscription you purchased (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). RevenueCat may process data in the USA; transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, we do not track you across apps or websites, we do not sell data, and we do not require you to create an account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your data, to data portability and to object. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Since we hold almost no data about you, most requests concern RevenueCat; contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
