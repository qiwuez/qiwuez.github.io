Privacy Policy

Save Your Chick: Push Up Game (on your home screen: "Pushup Game") · Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short
  • Your camera image is analysed on your iPhone only. It is never sent to us. A short video of your round stays on your iPhone and only leaves it if you share or save it yourself.
  • There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • We count anonymous usage statistics (for example, which onboarding step people reach) to improve the app. They cannot be linked to you.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple and our payment partner RevenueCat.

Camera

The app uses your front camera to detect your push-ups. Each camera frame is processed in real time on your device with Apple's on-device body pose detection (Apple Vision). Camera images and body landmarks are never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else.

Share video

During a round, the app records what you see on screen (your camera image with the game on top) into a temporary video on your iPhone, so it can offer you a short clip to share at the end. Nothing is uploaded. The clip only leaves your device if you tap Share and pick an app or "Save Video" yourself; saving to Photos needs your permission. Otherwise the video is deleted when you close the share screen or start the next round.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, the name you give your chick, your answers to the onboarding questions, your push-up counts, streaks and game progress are stored locally on your iPhone. We have no access to them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules a daily reminder as a local notification on your device. No data is sent to us for this.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how the app is used and where it can be improved, the app sends anonymous usage events to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany), for example "onboarding step reached", your answers to the onboarding questions (such as your goal, your age and how many push-ups you can do), "camera access allowed", "round finished" with the number of push-ups, or "tab opened". TelemetryDeck also receives basic technical data such as device model, iOS version, app version and language. Events contain no name, no email address, no advertising identifier, no camera images and no free text you enter (such as your chick's name). The device identifier is hashed on your device and again on TelemetryDeck's servers, so it cannot be traced back to you or your device. The data is processed in the EU and is not combined with other data or used for advertising. Legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. To manage your subscription status we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and basic technical device data (such as app version, iOS version, country and language). This is necessary to provide the subscription you purchased (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). RevenueCat may process data in the USA; transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Feedback and ratings

If you send us feedback by email, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. Ratings are handled by Apple.

What we do not do

We do not use advertising SDKs, we do not track you across apps or websites, we do not sell data, and we do not require you to create an account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your data, to data portability and to object. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Since we hold almost no data about you, most requests concern RevenueCat or TelemetryDeck; contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
