How do I set up my phone?

Place your iPhone flat on the floor, screen up, and get into push-up position above it. Your head and shoulders should be visible to the front camera. Good light helps.

My push-ups are not counted

A push-up counts when your shoulders go down far enough toward the phone. Go a bit deeper, make sure your shoulders are in view, and avoid strong light from behind you.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In the app: Settings → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge.

I bought a subscription but the app asks me to pay again

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my camera image uploaded?

No. Your camera image is analysed on your iPhone only. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
